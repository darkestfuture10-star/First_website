Welcome my personal space for the things I build, experiment with, and learn along the way.


I'm a Software Engineering student and developer interested in AI, software development, cybersecurity, automation, and technology. EasyMade is where I document my projects, from ideas that are still on the drawing board to tools and applications that are already up and running.

My current focus includes building Discord bots, exploring AI integrations, developing useful software tools, and gradually moving deeper into cybersecurity. I enjoy taking an idea, figuring out how it works, and turning it into something practical. Not everything here is perfect or finished. Some projects are experiments, some are learning experiences, and some may eventually be discontinued. That's part of the process.

I also have a gaming side through Part-Time Gamer (PTG), where I create gaming content, explore mods, and share the games I enjoy. EasyMade represents a simple idea: technology doesn't always have to be complicated. I want to build things that are useful, interesting, and easy to understand while continuously improving my skills along the way.
